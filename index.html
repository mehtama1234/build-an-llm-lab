Real run · GPT-2 124M

Unpacking a neuron's many meanings

Train a sparse autoencoder on real GPT-2 activations to undo superposition: 32 features rebuild the 768-number vector (R²=0.90), and they come apart into nameable features — a passive-verb feature, a start-of-line feature, the comma, the word 'the' — where raw coordinates were gibberish.

Real run · GPT-2 124M

The same word, two meanings

Watch one fixed token grow two meanings in real GPT-2: the word 'bank' starts identical in 'river bank' and 'bank account' (0.99) and pulls apart layer-by-layer (to 0.26), each drifting toward its real sense — river words vs money words. Static embeddings in, contextual meaning out.

A guess, with odds

The model's real output is a probability over ~50,000 tokens: peaked when forced ('Thank you very'→much, 0.1 bits) vs flat when open (8.6 bits). See temperature reshape the odds and greedy-vs-sampling turn the same model from a repetitive parrot into a varied writer.
